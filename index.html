About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Hospital das Clínicas, University of São Paulo, working at the intersection of transcriptomics, metabolomics, and computational diagnostics. My research asks a simple question in a complex way: how do we turn the enormous amount of multi-omics data science now generates into tools that actually reach the people who need them — a clinician deciding on a diagnosis, a policymaker deciding on a climate intervention.

Every research programme I build starts from the same observation: rich multi-omics knowledge exists in the literature, and rarely reaches deployable tools.

My path runs through Nigeria, Malaysia, and Brazil — three research environments that shape how I think about building tools that work outside of ideal, well-resourced conditions. See the full portfolio →

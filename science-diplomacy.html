Science Diplomacy

Connecting research systems in Nigeria and Brazil.

I am a Nigerian biochemist working at the University of São Paulo. Alongside my research, I work to strengthen scientific partnerships between Nigeria, Brazil and the wider Global South.

Research Partnerships Capacity Building Evidence for Policy

Why this bridge

On 25 August 2025, Nigeria and Brazil signed a memorandum of understanding on science, technology and innovation, covering biotechnology, the bioeconomy, ocean science, energy, space, digital transformation and new materials. Agreements like this succeed when researchers on both sides know each other, trust each other's institutions, and can turn shared priorities into funded projects.

Good partnerships are built on evidence both sides can check.

My own path runs through Nigeria, Malaysia and Brazil — three research systems with different resources, rules and strengths. That experience shapes how I think about partnerships that work for both sides, not just on paper.
